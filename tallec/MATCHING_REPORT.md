# Movers against matched stayers

Exploratory. Generated by `matching_moves.py`. Ratings are season-only peer scores (50 = position-group median of that competition and season) from one table built through 2026. Intervals: 95% player-cluster bootstrap over movers, with matches held fixed. An independent review re-matched inside the bootstrap (resampling movers and stayers): pair-gap intervals come out 1.28-1.53 times wider, so read these as 30-50% too narrow (NRL-SL about +/-7.9 rather than +/-5.1).

**Selection on promotions.** NSW Cup players promoted to the NRL who also played 3+ NSW Cup matches in their landing season rated +17.1 [+10.0, +23.8] above their matched stayers in the NSW Cup that same season (Queensland Cup: +10.3): clubs promote players who are better than their season-before profile shows. That biases the matched NRL-Cup gaps toward zero by several points; the AKM estimate (`AKM_REPORT.md`) is the more credible one for the Cups.

Movers rated on both sides: 804; matched: 526; dropped, no stayer within the caliper: 278. Stayer pool: 3,988 player-seasons. Median matches per mover: 2 of 5.

## Placebos

- **Season two before, not matched on**: movers' change from t−2 to t−1 minus their stayers' change, 231 movers with both seasons: -0.45 [-2.48, +1.74]. Zero means movers were not on a different trajectory from stayers who looked the same at t−1.
- **Pretend move inside NRL**: 218 stayers, matched to other stayers: +0.31 (se 1.88). Should be zero.
- **Pretend move inside NSW**: 68 stayers, matched to other stayers: -4.37 (se 3.67). Should be zero.
- **Pretend move inside QLD**: 166 stayers, matched to other stayers: -3.24 (se 1.91). Should be zero.
- **Pretend move inside SL**: 216 stayers, matched to other stayers: +2.54 (se 1.70). Should be zero.
